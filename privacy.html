Privacy Policy

Driving Rules · Last updated August 2026

Driving Rules is a study app for US driver's license and learner's permit tests. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

We do not collect personal information

Driving Rules does not ask for an account, a name, an email address, or a phone number. There is no sign-up and no login. We cannot identify you.

Your data stays on your device

All driving rules, road signs and practice questions are stored inside the app itself. Your selected state, bookmarks, and study progress are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a diagnostic report containing:

  • the type of crash and where it occurred in the app
  • your device model and iOS version
  • which US state you had selected in the app, to help reproduce the problem
  • a randomly generated installation identifier

These reports are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. See Firebase's privacy information for how Google handles this data.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics profiles

Driving Rules contains no advertising, no third-party trackers, and does not sell or share any information.

Links to official DMV websites

The app can open your state's official driver handbook on that state's own website. Those sites are operated by the relevant state agency and have their own privacy policies. We do not receive any information about what you do there.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
