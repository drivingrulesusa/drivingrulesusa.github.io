Driving Rules USA

Study for your US driver's license and learner's permit test.

Driving Rules is an iOS study companion covering all 50 states and Washington, D.C. Every rule comes from that state's official DMV driver handbook.

  • 3,458 rules taken from official state driver handbooks
  • 4,080 practice questions — 80 for every state
  • 641 US road signs with what each one means
  • Works fully offline, with no account and no sign-up
